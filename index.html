A RestedXP custom guide for WoW Forever launch day. It covers Valley of Trials (1-5) and Durotar (5-11), then hands off to RXP's own 12-17 The Barrens guide. Built for Herbalism and Cooking, avoiding quest bottlenecks; Tailoring is trained, but only the recipes the route uses.

Install

  1. Install the RestedXP Guides addon (RXPGuides). This guide requires it.
  2. Copy the RXP_DurotarLaunchDate folder from the download into World of Warcraft\<version folder>\Interface\AddOns\. It must contain the .toc and the .lua file.
  3. Restart the game. A /reload is not enough for a new addon.
  4. Open RXP, choose Custom/Group, and select Durotar Launch Date v1.0.
  5. Run 1-5 Valley of Trials first. It continues to 5-11 Durotar and then to RXP's Barrens guide.

Horde Orc Mage only; Alliance characters load nothing. The .toc targets Interface 16001; change it if your client build differs.

Things to know

  • Group content is skipped by design. Dark Storms (Fizzle Darkstorm) and the Ukorsbane pack are optional and need help.
  • Softcore deathskips. The #softcore steps ask you to die on purpose to respawn at a nearer graveyard. Hardcore players should skip them. Each prints a "Release: x, y" line so you can check the release point first.
  • Boosted Rest. The Razor Hill campfire sit gives the once-per-hour Boosted Rest buff; the second sit only helps if an hour has passed.
  • Gold. The route buys Spring Water, Conjure Water training and Tailoring recipes. Skip any you don't want.
  • Quest IDs 96xxx-99xxx are Forever-specific quests.

What is measured and what is estimated. Filled dots come from the guide's own coordinates; grind stops sit at the middle of the guide's loop waypoints. Hollow dots (~ in the table) are the Sen'jin and Razor Hill graveyards and the Echo Isles death spot, which the guide does not place. Run times are straight-line estimates and skip swimming speed.

Route notes

  • Order. Valley of Trials; one Sen'jin trip at 5 for the Kolkar plans; north past the ridge to Razor Hill; Tiragarde Keep with a tower deathskip; the shipwrecks and shore grind, then drown to Sen'jin for Frostbolt Rank 2 and the Echo Isles; back to Razor Hill for the Razormane camps and Threat from Below; Drygulch to 5875/6500; Orgrimmar for the flight path, auction house, Tailoring and Grommash Hold; Skull Rock for the Insignia and the Spitelash coast to 6725/7600; hearth to Razor Hill and Far Watch Post.
  • Level gates. 5 before Sen'jin. 2100/2800 in Kolkar so the Kolkar turn-in dings 6 (Fireball Rank 2, Fire Blast at Un'Thuwa). 3875/4500 at 7 on the shore so A Solvent Spirit dings 8 in Sen'jin. 9 for A Missing Hand. 5875/6500 at 9 in Drygulch so The Admiral's Orders at Nazgrel dings 10 (otherwise grind to 10). 6725/7600 at 10 on the coast so the Threat from Below turn-in puts you near 11 for the Barrens.
  • Training. Arcane Intellect and Frostbolt in the Valley (Frostbolt catch-up at Un'Thuwa); Conjure Water at Sen'jin; Fireball Rank 2 and Fire Blast at Un'Thuwa after the Kolkar turn-in; Frostbolt Rank 2 at Un'Thuwa at 8; Frost Nova and Conjure Water Rank 2 at Pephredo at 10.
  • Professions. Herbalism from Galgar's Cactus Apple reward; pick herbs on your path only. Cooking at Cook Torka. Keep all Linen Cloth: 40 by Orgrimmar for Tailoring (bolts, 4 Linen Bags to 25, then the robe, gloves and pants). Camping 101: Herbalism goes to Jandi in the Drag.
  • Deathskips. Kolkar camp to Sen'jin, Tiragarde's northern tower to Razor Hill, drowning south of the keep to Sen'jin, and the Echo Isles back to Sen'jin. Softcore only; check the Release macro before you die.
  • Hidden Enemies. Loot the Lieutenant's Insignia in Skull Rock and keep it. You turn it in to Thrall when the Barrens guide sends you back to Orgrimmar.
  • Optional. Tiragarde is skippable when crowded. A Solvent Spirit never blocks you. Lost in the Shadows only if Pal'juh is up. Ukorsbane only with a group. Securing the Lines only if you are short on XP. Dark Storms (Fizzle Darkstorm, level 12) only with a group; turn it in to Orgnil at the end.
  • Skipped. Practical Prey, and the elite Aggor follow-up unless you have a group.
  • Off the zone map. Orgrimmar stops come from the guide's Orgrimmar coordinates and sit north of the Durotar map edge, so the full-route map clips them; the Orgrimmar plate shows them. Far Watch Post is just over the Barrens border.
